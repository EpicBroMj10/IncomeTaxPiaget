Section 01 · The Name
English · Computing
NULL
Zero. The empty state.

In computing, NULL is not an error — it is a deliberate declaration: this value has not yet been assigned. NUUL starts at zero. No pretence. No borrowed credibility. Starting from nothing. Choosing to.

Tamil · நூல்
NOOL
Thread. Origin material.

In Tamil, நூல் (nool) means thread — the fundamental material of everything NUUL makes. Also means a book: knowledge woven together. Sounds Tamil. Reads global. Both correct. Always.

NUUL

Top — Neodymium N52 Magnets
Auto-Aligns. Auto-Holds.

8mm × 3mm discs, fabric-encased. Bring panel near tee back — it pulls itself into alignment automatically. No clicking. Cold wash safe. Never tumble dry.

Bottom — POM Snaps + Finger Flaps
Touch the Flap. Find the Snap.

20mm POM snaps behind fabric tabs that look like design elements. Insert finger — snap releases. One motion. 3-5 seconds.
